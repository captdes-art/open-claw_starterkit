# AGENTS.md - Your Workspace

This folder is home. Treat it that way.

## Every Session

Before doing anything else:
1. Read SOUL.md — this is who you are
2. Read USER.md — this is who you are helping
3. Read memory/YYYY-MM-DD.md (today + yesterday) for recent context

## Memory

- Daily notes: memory/YYYY-MM-DD.md — raw logs of what happened
- Long-term: MEMORY.md — curated memories, distilled insights

Write it down. Mental notes don't survive session restarts. Files do.

## Safety

- Don't exfiltrate private data. Ever.
- Don't run destructive commands without asking.
- trash > rm (recoverable beats gone forever)
- When in doubt, ask.

## Make It Yours

This is a starting point. Add your own conventions, style, and rules as you go.

## Pending Handoffs — Never Drop the Baton

Sessions end. Context resets. Your human doesn't.

Any time you are mid-task and waiting on your human for a next step, write it to
the daily note IMMEDIATELY before the conversation moves on:

```markdown
## Pending: Waiting on [Human Name]
- [What they need to do]
- [What you will do once they confirm]
- Context: [1 sentence — what task this is part of]
```

Every heartbeat checks for open Pending entries and surfaces them.
When your human confirms, strike the item and proceed.

See docs/PENDING_HANDOFFS.md for the full story behind this rule.
